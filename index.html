Sales Analysis

01

Excel · Sales data · Profit

I audited 113,000 sales records and built an Excel dashboard showing which products kept more profit and how discounts affected margins.

More about this project

Auditing the numbers

I analyzed 113,000 sales records to find which products earned more profit and how discounts affected margins.

During the data quality audit, I identified fabricated duplicate rows that copied earlier years with altered figures and flagged them so they were excluded from the clean analysis while remaining traceable in the workbook.

What discounts showed

Full price sales had a 51% profit margin. Sales with discounts above 15% had a 31% margin, showing how steep discounting quietly eroded profit.

Helmets delivered a 59% margin, demonstrating that higher unit prices did not always mean higher profitability.

Why it matters

The interactive Excel dashboard highlights top-performing products and discount thresholds, giving stakeholders a clear starting point when reviewing pricing and promotions.

Online Sales Analysis

03

SQL · Power BI · Sales trends

I used SQL and Power BI across 131,000+ records to uncover why revenue fell, where cancelled orders concentrated and which categories still grew.

More about this project

Where were sales falling?

I analyzed over 131,000 sales records using MySQL for deduplication and standardization and Power BI for data modeling, DAX measures and interactive reporting.

One product group stood out

Monthly sales fell by 20.6%, and a single category accounted for 81% of the drop, making it the top priority to investigate.

Three product categories generated 91% of total revenue, highlighting a heavy concentration risk across the catalog.

Cancelled orders and growth areas

The dashboard isolates cancellation and return hotspots by location and fulfillment channel so operations teams know where to focus.

At the same time, one apparel category grew by 27%, and B2B buyers placed larger orders with lower cancellation rates than individual buyers.

Research & Report Automation

04

n8n · Multi-agent · PDF reports

I built a four-workflow multi-agent system in n8n that searches the web, verifies quotations against source pages and generates a cited PDF report with comparison charts.

More about this project

Preparing research briefs manually requires gathering sources, cross-checking figures and formatting charts and citations into a clean document.

I architected four coordinated n8n workflows: an orchestrator that creates a factual checklist, a research agent that extracts web sources via Tavily and runs code checks to verify exact quotations, an analyst workflow that validates coverage and generates QuickChart visuals, and a report writer that compiles HTML into a finished PDF via Gotenberg and uploads it to Google Drive.

On a benchmark run comparing 2010 and 2020 U.S. Census population figures, the pipeline completed in 87 seconds and delivered a two-page cited PDF report with a comparison table and bar chart.

AI Voice Receptionist

05

ElevenLabs · n8n · Google Calendar · Vonage

I built a bilingual AI voice receptionist in English and Egyptian Arabic that answers service questions, checks Google Calendar availability, books appointments and sends SMS confirmations.

More about this project

Service businesses spend hours answering repetitive calls, checking schedules and logging customer requests across separate calendars.

I built a bilingual voice receptionist combining an ElevenLabs conversational voice agent with a 66-node n8n backend workflow. It handles calls in English and Egyptian Arabic, validates business hours and service durations, checks availability across separate cleaning and repair Google Calendars, and books confirmed appointments.

Once an appointment is created on the calendar, the workflow logs customer and booking records in Google Sheets, sends an SMS confirmation with the appointment date and time via Vonage, and routes complex issues or refund requests to a staff follow-up log.
